VineTrack Sync — Chrome Web Store listing draft

Name
VineTrack Sync

Short description
Sync visible Amazon Vine items into your VineTrack account with an explicit click. No Amazon password required.

Detailed description
VineTrack Sync is the optional Chrome extension for VineTrack. It helps Vine reviewers move visible product details from their Amazon Vine Orders or Reviews page into their private VineTrack workflow.

How it works:
• Sign in to VineTrack.
• Connect the extension once.
• Open an Amazon Vine Orders or Reviews page.
• Click “Sync My Vine Items”.
• VineTrack opens and imports the synced batch.

Privacy and review integrity:
• The extension does not ask for or store your Amazon password.
• It does not run background scraping.
• It reads visible item details only after you click Sync.
• It does not choose star ratings.
• It does not submit Amazon reviews.
• VineTrack review drafts remain based on your own testing notes and observations.
